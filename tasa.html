What is TASA?

A loving community brought together by shared cultural experiences.

As a TASA Fam Head, we host events to curate a space where members can feel connected and have fun. We prioritize giving our members a place outside of class and other commitments to come together and take a break from everyone's busy schedules. With monthly events and gatherings, we hope to foster a home away from home for students to find their forever friends.

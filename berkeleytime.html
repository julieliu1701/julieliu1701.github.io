The Challenge

Increasing adoption of new features.

In October 2026, Berkeleytime dropped its newest feature, GradTrak, allowing students to plan out their graduation requirements. However, usership in the first few months was slow. Based on user feedback collected from surveys, most students were either unaware of the new feature, unsure of how to use the new feature, or preferred to use their own preexisting methods of tracking their graduation requirements. Some of these methods included using Google Sheets, Excel, Notion, and other organization tools in preference to GradTrak, despite GradTrak being specifically geared toward helping Berkeley students.

Our Strategy

Gathering user feedback and promoting new features.

To increase usership on GradTrak, we targeted outreach to our social media presence. For the students who were unaware or unsure of how to use the new feature, we created guides detailing how to use those features. Designing graphics for carousel posts, as well as filming and editing videos directed toward incoming freshmen acclimating to college and current students still in the process of choosing classes, we were able to reach more students, promoting the new feature while growing our social media presence.

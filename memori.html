The Challenge

Building social media traction.

Since Memori’s public launch, transitioning out of stealth in late 2025, the company has faced fierce competitors in the field of agentic memory infrastructure. Similar startups like Zep and Letta, also building persistent memory and context management for LLMs and AI agents, having launched in March 2024 and September 2024 respectively, had an advantage over Memori simply for launching earlier, despite Memori offering better accuracy rates and lower inference costs.

Our Strategy

Leveraging social media trends, timely topics in tech, and short-form content.

As Memori’s first cohort of student ambassadors, we aimed to help Memori increase its usership and status by growing the company’s social media presence through short-form content and networking events. By capitalizing on current social media trends and targeting popular topics in the tech space, we were able to generate traction on our Instagram and Youtube platforms. Meanwhile, leveraging our LinkedIn networks allowed us to gain support within the platform’s tech/business-oriented community.
